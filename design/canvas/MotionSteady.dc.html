MOTION RULES

No shape morphing: only transform, opacity and trim paths animate. Travel under 180 pt, scale between 0.7 and 1.1.

Easing: ease-in-out (0.35, 0, 0.65, 1) for movement, ease-out (0.2, 0, 0.3, 1) for things arriving. Reduce Motion: show a still frame with .currentProgress(0.6).

Colors are baked in from the system tokens. For Dark Mode, swap fills with a ColorValueProvider (paper #1F1E1A, tint #0F2E22).
